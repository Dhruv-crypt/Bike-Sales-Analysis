# 🚴 Bike Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

A data-driven Power BI dashboard analyzing 6 years (2011–2016) of bike sales to uncover growth trends, customer behavior, and revenue optimization opportunities.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores sales performance across multiple countries, demographics, and product categories, highlighting key business insights and strategic opportunities in the bicycle market.

* Total Revenue:** $43,396
* Net Profit:** $16,768
* YoY Growth:** 336%
* Geographies:** USA, UK, Germany, France, Canada, Australia

---

## 🚀 Key Insights

* Explosive Growth:** 336% YoY growth indicates strong post-pandemic demand for fitness and outdoor mobility
* Accessories = Profit Driver:** Low revenue share but high-margin and repeat purchase potential
* Demographic Risk:** 65–75% revenue comes from adults (35–64), indicating future market risk
* Gender Balance:** Near 50/50 male-female split reflects a mature and inclusive market

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* KPI Cards (Revenue, Profit, Growth)
* Sales by Product Category and Gender
* Age Group vs Product Analysis
* Country-wise Sales (Map Visualization)
* Yearly Trends and Seasonal Patterns
* Top-Selling Products Analysis
